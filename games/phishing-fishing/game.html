How to verify

Every address in this game uses a fictional .example domain. That ending means training, not phishing. These are the services' reference domains, not real website addresses.

Check the whole destination. A subdomain such as events.xmum.example belongs under xmum.example. A name such as xmum.example.other.example belongs under other.example.

Sender addresses can be spoofed and legitimate accounts can be compromised. A matching domain, HTTPS or polished formatting does not prove authenticity. A deadline or payment amount does not prove phishing. Check the request and use a known app, bookmark or contact to verify it independently.

During a round, the shared timer keeps running while this reference is open.
