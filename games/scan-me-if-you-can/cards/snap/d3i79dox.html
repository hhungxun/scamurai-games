What does a tracking number look like?

Pos Laju tracking numbers are typically 13 characters long:

Two lettersFollowed by nine digitsEnding with MY

For example: EN123456789MY

The opening letters indicate the type of service used, while "MY" confirms Malaysia as the origin country.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I track my parcel with Pos Malaysia?

Simply enter your tracking number at the top of the page. You'll see real-time status updates for your shipment, including location checkpoints and estimated delivery. Your tracking number can be found on your shipping receipt or in the confirmation email from your seller.

Can I use this tool for international tracking?

Yes. This tracking tool works for both domestic and international shipments sent through Pos Malaysia, including international express (EMS) and Prepaid Flexipack Package Envelopes. Just enter the tracking number as usual and the system will recognise the service type automatically.

Does Pos Malaysia offer overseas shipping tracking to all countries?

Pos Malaysia provides overseas shipping tracking for parcels sent to over 230 countries and territories. However, tracking visibility may vary depending on the destination country's postal network. For detailed updates, you can also contact our customer service team on 1300-300-300.

Can I track an international letter sent through Pos Malaysia?

International letter tracking is available for items sent via International Registered Mail. Standard international letters without registration are not trackable. If you need end-to-end visibility, registered or express options are recommended.

What should I do if my tracking status hasn't updated?

It can take up to 24 hours for the system to refresh, especially during peak periods or when parcels are clearing customs. If there's been no update for more than three working days, reach out to us via AskPos or call 1300-300-300 for assistance. You can also check our scam update page to verify any suspicious delivery messages.
