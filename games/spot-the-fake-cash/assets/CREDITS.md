# Spot the Fake Cash assets

- **3D game** comes from the PHYSC science camp's `06-station-games/spot-the-fake-cash/original-vr-chatgpt7.html`. The port keeps the scene, note shader, UV torch and WebXR controls.
- **Three.js r128** is vendored at `../vendor/three.min.js` from the [cdnjs r128 distribution](https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js). See the [included MIT license](../vendor/three.LICENSE).
- **Note combinations and banknote images** (12 JPEGs in `notes/`) come from the station owner's latest version and asset pack (Nov 2026). They are used unaltered apart from renaming.
