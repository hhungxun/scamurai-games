# Artwork and references

The inspection samples are original geometric diagrams created for Scamurai in HTML and CSS. The game contains no photographs, artwork, portraits, logos, or layouts taken from genuine notes. No third-party currency image or licence is in use. The generic wave, circle, star, stripe and lettering show the difference between passing and failing checks without matching any particular note design.

- [Bank Negara Malaysia, Checking Currency Authenticity](https://www.bnm.gov.my/currency/authentication) describes Feel, Look, Tilt and Check, including UV and magnification.
- [Bank Negara Malaysia, fourth-series RM10 security features](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/currency/banknotes/rm10) documents the watermark portrait and numeral, see-through numeral, UV Rafflesia with numeral, micro-lettering, raised printing and glossy stripe. The game cites these as real-world examples in the debrief.
- [Bank Negara Malaysia, Current Banknote Series](https://www.bnm.gov.my/currency/banknotes) lists the current denominations.
- [Bank Negara Malaysia, Guidelines on Reproduction of Malaysian Currency Image](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/39730/en_reproduction_guide.pdf) §§1.3–1.4, 8.1, 8.3 and 9 provide general approval only for qualifying reproductions. Electronic banknote images must preserve the original design and use the original colours or black and white. The rules permit scaling or slanting, with the ruler and other imagery protected from distortion. Reproductions must preserve the currency’s dignity and integrity. Digital-only §8.3 sets no DPI, size threshold, single-side or SPECIMEN requirement. Printed copies and merchandise have separate conditions in §§8.2 and 8.4, including one-sided printing, both dimensions at most two-thirds or at least 1.5 times the original, and bold diagonal SPECIMEN or CONTOH with length at least one-third of the reproduction's length and height at least one-eighth of its width. Those sections also impose substrate conditions. Altering a real note image to simulate a defect would require specific approval.
- [BNM website terms](https://www.bnm.gov.my/terms-of-use) restrict redistribution and modification. [Wikimedia Commons’ Malaysia currency guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Copyright_rules_by_territory/Malaysia#Currency) flags reuse restrictions. We did not identify a verified reusable current-series banknote image.
