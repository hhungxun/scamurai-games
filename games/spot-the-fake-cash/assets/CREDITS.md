# Spot the Fake Cash assets

- **3D game and note combinations** come from the PHYSC science camp's `06-station-games/spot-the-fake-cash/original-vr-chatgpt7.html`. The port keeps the scene, note shader, UV torch and WebXR controls.
- **Three.js r128** is vendored at `../vendor/three.min.js` from the [cdnjs r128 distribution](https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js). See the [included MIT license](../vendor/three.LICENSE).
- **Banknote images** are supplied by the station owner. The [station README](../README.md) lists the required filenames and current inventory. The station currently uses labeled canvas placeholders drawn by the game.
